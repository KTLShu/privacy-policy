1. 服务说明与软件特性

【一叶文档搜索】是一款面向 Android 设备的纯本地离线文档全文检索与工作台工具。本软件的核心功能包括：

  • 全盘索引与文件名秒搜：后台增量扫描设备文档并构建本地索引，支持按格式、修改时间、文件大小与名称快速筛选排序；
  • 本地文档正文全文检索：支持对 PDF、Word (.docx/.doc)、Excel (.xlsx/.xls/.csv/.tsv)、PPT (.pptx/.ppt) 及纯文本/代码 (.txt/.md/.json 等) 进行离线关键字检索与上下文高亮定位；
  • 常用目录与受限目录适配：聚合下载、文档及常用社交/办公应用目录，支持在用户明确授权后通过 SAF 或 Shizuku 访问特定目录；
  • 内置文本预览与轻量编辑：支持多种字符编码自动探测与切换，以及纯文本文件的本地预览和保存；
  • 100% 离线本地运行：全部索引、解析与检索计算均在您的设备本地执行，绝不上传您的文档或关键词。

Leaf Doc Search is an offline-first document indexing, full-text search, and lightweight text viewing/editing utility for Android. Key capabilities include:

  • Local Document Indexing & Filename Filtering: Incrementally indexes local documents on-device for instant filtering and sorting;
  • Offline Full-Text Search: Parses and searches keywords locally inside PDF, Word (.docx/.doc), Excel (.xlsx/.xls/.csv/.tsv), PowerPoint (.pptx/.ppt), and plain-text/code files (.txt/.md/.json, etc.) with highlighted context snippets;
  • Smart Directory Scopes: Aggregates Downloads, Documents, and user-authorized messaging/work app folders (via SAF or optional Shizuku);
  • Built-in Text Viewer & Editor: Detects character encodings and supports local viewing and saving of plain-text files;
  • 100% Local Processing: All indexing, extraction, and text operations execute on your device without uploading files or keywords.
